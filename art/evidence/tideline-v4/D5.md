# D5 — Pump hall and audio evidence

The explicit `?demoPumpHall=1` switch takes the opened hall on lap three. `?demoPumpHall=0` holds the main-road alternative for comparison. The accepted default remains the pump-hall route, preserving the baseline driver outcome.

`audio.mjs` captures the live driver's actual position, rather than the slower diagnostics display. `pump-hall-capture.json` records three requested stations, the live progress and lap, craft position, and shortcut state. All three corrected frames are on the lap-three shortcut.

`audio/drain-transition.wav` is the real Web Audio master during the full-speed demo drain transition. `audio/pump-hall-stationary.wav` is a stationary listening position in the drained hall with the engine idling and normal audio clock. It is explicitly not a slowed or looped race pass: a normal pass is shorter than the requested listening sample. The dry hall now keeps its enclosure acoustics.

`audio-metrics.py` verifies exactly 20 seconds and 10 seconds of delivered 48 kHz stereo PCM, respectively, with no clipped samples. `audio.mjs` captures the actual browser bus through MediaRecorder; ffmpeg decodes and trims by sample count. Original WebM captures are retained. The metrics prove non-silence and delivery duration, not subjective musical or ambience quality.
