# Tideline V4 — final sky turntable visual review

Reviewed only the current `sky-turntable/view-00.png` through `view-23.png`, opening every numbered image. No source, capture metadata, keys, implementation reports, or browser state informed this review.

**Verdict: the turntable passes visual continuity, with a qualification on the specific blue-hour mood.** It reads as one coherent overcast industrial dusk. I do not see a moon, a hard warm/night or warm/cold split between directions, or a conspicuously pasted horizon. I would not call the blue-hour appearance unequivocal: the pale blue-gray sky, brown horizon haze, and strong amber road lighting make the overall impression closer to gray industrial evening than a distinctly blue evening.

Visible observations:

- The upper sky keeps the same pale blue-gray cloud cover around the turn. No view changes into a separate night sky, sunset hemisphere, or star field.
- The brown-gray horizon haze remains consistent. Distant refinery silhouettes lose contrast into it rather than ending at an obvious cutout edge.
- Warm road, arch, and tank highlights persist through the rotation. Their contrast with the cool sky is a consistent scene-lighting choice, not a directional sky discontinuity.
- I see no moon or other bright celestial disc in the 24 supplied views.
- The horizon is low contrast and partly occluded by the track structure. This helps its integration but limits how much fine horizon detail these images expose. The captures support a visual continuity finding, not proof that every possible camera elevation is seamless.

A separate scene limitation remains visible below the skyline: the almost-black basin contains flat, sharply bounded turquoise-green patches, particularly noticeable beside the tanks in the later views. These can read as thin polygonal puddles against an unusually dark surface. That is a ground/water presentation issue; it does not look like a pasted sky horizon or a change of time of day.

Only this review artifact was written; no implementation or other evidence was changed.
