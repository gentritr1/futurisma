# D2 — one render rule

Executed: Tideline course markers and gameplay signals use lit Lambert materials. The Tideline-only vehicle/fleet adapter retains live color/opacity controls and existing shader customizations, promotes unlit mesh materials to Lambert, and routes custom emission shaders through AgX and scene fog. Other course paths do not call the adapter. No lighting or pace changes were made to other maps.

Observed: `scripts/visual/tideline-v4/smoke.mjs` first inspected 128 material bindings, including hidden player/fleet meshes. The final `race.mjs` walk is authoritative for the completed scene: its individual object names, material types, fog and tone-mapping flags are in `final-race/material-walk.json` and `reduced-race/material-walk.json`. These are structural checks, separate from visual acceptance.

Executed visual acceptance: `fog.mjs` captures ten families beside the actual Tidal Pump Gantry at 45, 100 and 180 metres, with a fixed camera height, a 20-degree lens, the same warm lights, AgX exposure and drained-world fog. Families are cradle, Surge, Shield, strip, cable lane, bulkhead, gate markers, refund dome, launch entrance marker and current entrance marker. The lane is one real module, and the gate is one pair, so unrelated route copies cannot confound the comparison.

Observed by the reviewer without source access: all final families recede compatibly with the gantry. The authoritative review chain is `fog-distance/independent-review-final.md` for bulkhead/gate/dome, `final-refresh-review.md` for the refreshed devices/strip/lane, and `marker-review.md` for the final cradle and both overhead markers. The earlier lane occlusion was resolved and reviewed in `lane-review-final.md`; the final refresh also checks it. Bright contrasting paint survives farther than small dark machinery, but no assembly was identified as visibly bypassing the world's fog or lighting.

Scope: this is visual compatibility in the supplied comparison views, not a reviewer proving shader equivalence. The live material walk supplies the complementary structural assertion. Earlier failed/confounded captures and review notes are retained and are not used as final acceptance.

Commands (Node 20.19.4 on PATH): `node scripts/visual/tideline-v4/fog.mjs`, then the normal and reduced `race.mjs` commands listed in the delivery README.
