# D2 — one render rule

Executed: Tideline course markers and gameplay signals use lit Lambert materials. The Tideline-only vehicle/fleet adapter retains live color/opacity controls and existing shader customizations, promotes unlit mesh materials to Lambert, and routes custom emission shaders through AgX and scene fog. Other course paths do not call the adapter. No lighting or pace changes were made to other maps.

Observed: `scripts/visual/tideline-v4/smoke.mjs` inspected 128 material bindings in the actual running renderer, including hidden player/fleet meshes. All had tone mapping enabled and fog enabled. The browser reported no errors. `render-rule/material-walk.json` includes individual names and types. This is a structural and compilation check, not proof that the objects blend visually.

Pending final visual acceptance: the three-distance gantry comparison must use the completed D3 cradles, strips and doors, then receive a source-blind review. The final race audit will repeat the material walk after those assets are present.
