# D1 — continuous blue hour

Executed: two native sky generations, final full-image resampling to the delivery dimensions; horizon haze in the actual sky shader; a directional amber refinery rim, received by the world's lit surfaces. The sky uses AgX and exposure 1.04, matching gameplay. Reduced motion supplies a frozen sky time.

Observed: `sky-profile.py` measures the top 75% of the final panorama, including circular wrapping: warmth delta 0.006224 over ten degrees; column luma range 1.120434. `turntable.mjs` captures 24 views at 15° intervals at review station 3, lap 3, seed 3868938316, plus isolated sky passes with the identical camera and shader. `turntable-profile.py` measures the top 180 rows in those isolated passes: worst luma ratio across 128 pixels is 1.123467. Foreground ribs are excluded deliberately; they are not sky discontinuities. Raw views and measurements are in `sky-turntable/`.

Visually inspected opposite-facing actual scene frames: the cloud state remains continuous, silhouettes dissolve into brown-grey horizon haze, and the road/steel receive a warm rim. The broad flare glow is intentionally faint. The angular spread is an art interpretation, not a measured 140° physical light volume.

Resolution limitation: native image generation returned 1774×887 despite a 4096×1024 request. The delivered texture is 4096×1024 after resampling; it does not contain native 4K detail. Prompts and exact provenance are in `generation.json`; both native sky candidates are retained in `art/textures/tideline-v4/`.
