These are persistent fidelity differences, not visible loss of the five identity details.

## Shield

Five shared visible details remain:

1. A central segmented iris/shutter within a circular opening.
2. A surrounding circular flange with fasteners around its perimeter.
3. A P-12 identification plate on the upper-left portion of the housing, with corner fasteners.

The side actuators are simplified into broad forms, and the nearly uniform mottled surface reads less clearly as salvaged steel than the hero. The model's pale concrete-looking base differs from the reference's metal base. The cool beacon remains recognizable but less luminous and less rounded than the reference.
