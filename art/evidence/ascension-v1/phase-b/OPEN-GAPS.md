# Phase B is not accepted

This is a reviewable working art build from approved Phase A `2ebd9d5`. No Phase C work is claimed. The route and schedule are preserved.

1. **Long trench art gate fails.** The ten-station `trench-tour/` uses the accepted fork without shortening it. Repeated pipe/valve/ladder/lamp bays remain too uniform. The scorched subsection and pad overhead do not sufficiently sustain the full shortcut. Atlas repetition needs authored variation, not a route edit.
2. **Focal fidelity remains incomplete.** `focal/assessment.json` records observed shared details and per-asset failures. Crawler cabs/treads and rocket/platform/arm proportions are weak against the heroes; tanks and vent have stretched atlas detail; gravel bed and pier detail are insufficiently established at chase height. The comparisons are not approved. No independent reviewer or original-vehicle judgment has been obtained.
3. **Ground/water coverage and vegetation need revision.** Pale background gaps remain beside the late trench and exit. Mangrove canopies read as chunky repeated lobes. The second countdown board's housing is partly overlapped by foliage, although its time is legible in the saved frame.
4. **Reference compliance is incomplete.** Some generated sheets have inconsistent scale bars, view alignment or poses. Material-ID passes were generated but not all semantic assignments are approved (including rocket ceramic versus metal). The reference dimensions in the Blender script are authoritative for this working build; a generated scale bar is not a measurement. The crawler maquette itself has an ambiguous multi-view silhouette, so its comparison is not proof of a precise silhouette lock.
5. **Generation metadata and sky resolution limitations.** The built-in generator returned no service job IDs; `job_id:null` and actual output artifact IDs are recorded. The sky source is 1774×887, resampled by `sips` to 4096×1024; this is not a native 4096×1024 generation. Runtime profile and masked visible-sky checks pass, but the resolution deviation remains explicit.
6. **Device residual is only partly closed.** Both exported devices have emissive glass, small round rivets and metal bases, with saved close-ups. Shield lamp colour remains amber instead of the inherited cyan convention. Cradles are not implemented. No independent device-quality acceptance or at-speed pickup evidence exists.
7. **Launch-strip residual has physical evidence but lacks activation evidence.** The close-up shows a lens recessed inside a metal frame. Live Surge activation and the flash response are not wired into Ascension's current clock-only runtime.
8. **CHAIN residual is open.** There is no forced CHAIN demo flag, live reward or video. Player device activation, both phase bulkheads and cradles remain unimplemented. E is still consumed by the Phase A adapter. Rival power presentation is inherited, but this phase does not separately certify every rival firing.
9. **Finished-effects luma and speed readability are untested.** The painted base and pinned floors are retained alongside the blockout pair. The four schedule-state captures currently render static art, with no launch steam or deluge. They do not establish final steam readability or final luma compliance.
10. **Full event clearance remains untested.** The new static environment ray grid passes; the approved Phase A moving blockout sweep remains a separate result. Painted crawler spline motion, tread motion, full rocket ascent, launch-column clearance, entry-only animated rails, steam and smoke require Phase C. No claim that these are impossible or safe in motion is made.
11. **Remaining whole-level evidence is open.** The three-distance pasted-on comparison for every gameplay object, at-speed feature bursts, independent phase-blind test, event phase pairs, launch/trench audio, music duck and distance-delayed sound remain outstanding. Reduced-motion racing is tested here; invisible unimplemented effects cannot be certified by that run.
12. **Archive coverage remains unavailable.** `npm test` exits successfully but explicitly skips missing accepted Greenwater archives. Those archives were not validated. Small performance headroom in the painted build must be protected when powers and events are added.

These are acceptance gaps, not waived requirements. No claim of Phase B or whole-level completion should be based on this commit.
