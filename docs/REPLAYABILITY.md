# Replayability direction

Reviewed 2 October 2026. The Circuit Tour display is implemented in this pass.
The modes, mastery rewards and ability variants below are proposals, not shipped features.

## Build on the existing game

The game already has field races, reverse-grid sprints and solo time attack;
three rival tiers; saved records and supported-course ghosts; a three-contract
board; daily and weekly jobs; first-finish circuit bonuses; and garage craft,
parts and cosmetic rewards. Polarity, Tideline, Ascension and Dream Island
already have surge/shield systems. Frostline already has stabilizer and thermal
pickups. Adding another generic boost or another daily checklist would duplicate
these systems.

The launch screen now exposes the existing nine-circuit first-finish ledger.
It marks completed circuits, identifies the selected circuit and explains its
first-finish reward. Race settlement and persistence remain the source of truth;
demo runs do not progress the tour and a repeated finish does not repay the bonus.

## Recommended order

1. **Circuit mastery:** three authored objectives per map: finish, learn its
   distinctive mechanic, then improve a measured performance target. Show the
   next objective before launch and actual progress at results. Award a matching
   crew livery, number plate or restrained trail. Set medal times from human
   driving sessions rather than autopilot or arbitrary thresholds.
2. **Ghost duel / hotseat:** short solo runs with a rival's trace, immediate
   rematch and consistent craft setup. Start on courses with validated ghost
   support; Polarity's branching/inverted routes need explicit replay support.
   Local hotseat offers social competition before online networking exists.
3. **Elimination:** remove the last classified racer at clearly announced
   checkpoints after a full warm-up lap. Test against the current three bots
   first. A four-racer field needs few elimination rounds; never eliminate a
   player before they can learn a course. Separate mode records and rewards.
4. **Optional ability races:** prototype one meaningful choice at a time, with
   readable warning, counterplay and a shared starting loadout. Preserve the
   pure racing option. Multiplayer needs authoritative ability timing, pickup
   ownership, hit validation and reconnection before competitive play.
5. **Free season journey:** only once the above produces enough enjoyable
   variation. A proposed 20-tier journey could award cosmetics from the same
   nine service crews. Progress through any valid mode, offer catch-up, retain
   earned rewards, and archive previous journeys. Keep handling upgrades out
   of its reward track. A paid pass remains a separate commercial decision.

These are hypotheses to playtest, not promises of increased retention. Compare
voluntary rematches, session completion, map variety and return visits against
the existing build. Also collect whether players understood a failure and wanted
another attempt. Do not optimize a streak at the expense of the race itself.

## Distinct map challenges and possible ability experiments

| Circuit | Mastery objective to author | Optional experiment and tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greenwater | Clear the standing-water sector and steam approach without recovery. | Hydro skim: spend boost charge to reduce wet-surface drag briefly; cannot boost simultaneously. |
| Bitterpan | Read the conveyor lamps and cross the salt-drop sector cleanly. | Ballast brace: temporary crosswind resistance with reduced acceleration. |
| Night Shift | Chain clean gates through the expressway district. | Keep its pure-driving identity; reward precision sectors instead of adding weapons. |
| Polarity | Make a legal transfer and finish a measured upper/lower route challenge. | Tune existing surge versus shield choice around deck recharge; no redundant gravity button. |
| Tideline | Complete a flooded lap, then use the pump hall once it opens. | Survey pulse: preview the next open branch; information rather than an unconditional speed advantage. |
| Ascension | Follow the deluge diversion through the launch event. | Test shield timing against environmental exposure using the current ability rules before inventing another resource. |
| Dream Island | Improve a final-lap wet-causeway split after the clock strikes. | Observatory trial: fixed night conditions and equal craft setup for a short precision challenge. |
| Afterglow | Dodge a locked relay volley and finish without recovery. | Decoy: commit a false target before lock; early timing matters and it must not redirect an unavoidable hit onto another racer. |
| Frostline | Chain cleared snow lines and time the existing stabilizer/thermal pickups. | A thermal endurance variant built from current pickups, with clearly marked safe routes and no surprise traction changes. |

Every objective needs actual simulation events and explicit completion rules;
landmark animation must never award progress by itself. Cosmetic variants should
reuse the worn paint language and the circuit's service identity. Seasonal props
should leave braking markers, runoff and useful sightlines clear.

## Reference patterns

Trackmania's official Track of the Day documentation describes a curated map
spotlight paired with records and a knockout event. Its mapping guidance also
emphasizes recoverable checkpoints and readable routes for less experienced
drivers. That supports testing a focused featured circuit and accessible
elimination rules here, rather than scattering unrelated tasks across every map.
[Track of the Day documentation](https://doc.trackmania.com/play/what-is-totd/).

The December 2024 Weekly Shorts announcement describes short challenges,
medals and hotseat play. The useful pattern for this game is quick attempts at a
distinct driving problem with an easy rematch.
[Weekly Shorts announcement](https://www.trackmania.com/news/8472?lang=en).

Rocket League's August 2020 challenge-system announcement is a historical
example of combining onboarding, weekly and season challenges with rewards.
It is inspiration for clear goals and progression presentation, not evidence
that adding a pass alone improves retention in this game.
[Rocket League challenge-system announcement](https://www.rocketleague.com/news/rocket-league-challenges--a-closer-look/).
